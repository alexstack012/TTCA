## Adding an asset

For each new image, record:

1. Its repository path.
2. The creator and canonical source URL, where applicable.
3. The license, written permission, or other usage basis.
4. The exact attribution requested by the creator.
5. Whether modification and public redistribution are permitted.

Do not assume that an image being publicly searchable makes it reusable. Keep private campaign images
out of the deployed asset bundle until their status is documented.
